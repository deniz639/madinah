Can I give my zakat to Gaza?

Reviewed by Shaykh Hamza Idris · 10 Nov 2026
THE SHORT ANSWER

Yes, as long as it reaches people who are eligible for zakat, such as the poor and the needy. Give through campaigns marked zakat-eligible, where the charity keeps zakat separate and passes it only to eligible recipients.

Who can receive zakat?

The Qur’an (9:60) names eight groups, including the poor, the needy, people in debt and travellers in hardship. Families in Gaza who have lost their homes and income usually fall within the poor and the needy.

What to check before you give

The campaign shows the zakat-eligible badge, with the reviewer’s name and date.

The charity keeps zakat in a separate fund and reports how it was spent.

The aid goes to people, such as food parcels, cash support or medical care, rather than general running costs.
