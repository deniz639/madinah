Zakat questions

What is the nisab?+

The minimum amount of wealth you must hold for a lunar year before zakat is due. It is set by the value of 85g of gold or 595g of silver; scholars differ on which to use, and we show both.

Who can receive zakat?+

The eight categories named in the Qur’an (9:60), including the poor, the needy and those in debt. Campaigns marked zakat-eligible have been checked against these categories.

Does Madinah take any of my zakat?+

No. Madinah charges 0% on zakat. Card processing fees apply unless you choose to cover them.

How are charities rated?+

Our independent scholars’ board checks how each charity keeps, spends and reports zakat. Each rating shows the reviewer and the date.
