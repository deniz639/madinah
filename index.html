BEFORE YOU GIVE

Questions donors ask first.

Still unsure? Read the full help centre or ask our scholars.

Who are the charities on Madinah?+

Registered charities whose registration we check before any campaign goes live. Each profile shows its registration number, country and team.

Does Madinah take a cut?+

No platform fee. Card processing (about 3%) is deducted unless you choose to cover it, and you see it before you pay.

How do I know my zakat is handled correctly?+

Only campaigns our scholars’ board rates zakat-eligible can accept zakat. Each rating shows who reviewed it and when.

Can I give my zakat to Gaza?+

Yes, to campaigns that reach eligible recipients. Look for the zakat-eligible badge.

Will I get a receipt?+

Yes. Instantly by email and in your donor portal, plus a yearly giving summary you can download.

What if a campaign turns out to be fake?+

The Madinah Giving Guarantee: if a campaign is proven fake, your gift is refunded.

Can I give monthly, and cancel later?+

Yes. Choose monthly when you give, then pause or cancel any time from your donor portal.

Is zakat due on crypto?+

Most scholars treat crypto held as savings or for trade as zakatable wealth. Our Learn guide explains how to value it.
