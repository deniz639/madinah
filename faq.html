Questions, answered.

Can’t find it? Email support@madinah.com. We reply within one business day.

For donors

Is my donation secure?+

Payments are processed by a certified payment provider. Madinah never stores your full card number.

Will I get a tax receipt?+

Yes, where the charity is registered in your country. Receipts appear instantly in your donor portal.

Can I cancel a monthly gift?+

Yes. Pause, skip a month, change the amount or cancel any time in your donor portal.

Can I give anonymously?+

Yes. Tick “Give anonymously” and your name is hidden from the public supporters list.

For fundraisers

How do I start a fundraiser?+

Choose who you’re raising for, tell the story, set a goal and share. It takes about 5 minutes.

When do I get the money?+

Verified charities are paid weekly. Personal fundraisers are paid once identity checks are complete.

What can’t I raise money for?+

Anything illegal, misleading or not allowed under our policies. We review every campaign.

For charities

How do we get verified?+

Send your registration documents and bank details from the client portal. Most checks finish in 3 business days.

How does the zakat rating work?+

Our independent scholars’ board reviews how you keep, spend and report zakat, then publishes a rating with the reviewer and date.

What reports do we get?+

A live dashboard, a weekly report every Monday and a monthly strategy report, all in your client portal.
