Winter warmth for displaced families

by Hope Bridge Relief · Gaza and northern Syria

The need

Families displaced from their homes are facing a second winter in tents and damaged buildings. Night temperatures fall close to freezing, and many children have no warm clothing.

Meet Huda, mother of four

“The children sleep in their coats. A heater and blankets would mean they can sleep through the night.” Name changed and shared with consent.
