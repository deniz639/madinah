TRUST, GUARANTEE AND FEES

Every gift is checked, tracked and protected.

Here is exactly what happens between your donation and the people it helps.

01

We check the charity

Registration number checked with the regulator, bank account verified, people named.

02

Scholars rate the zakat

An independent board rates how zakat is kept, spent and reported.

03

We review every story

No outdated figures, no placeholders, no exaggerated claims. Stories are reviewed every 90 days.

04

Funds paid out weekly

Straight to the verified charity bank account, with a public payout record.

05

Updates within 30 days

Charities post photo or video proof. No update means no featured spot.

THE MADINAH GIVING GUARANTEE

If a campaign is proven fake, you get your money back.

Report a concern from any campaign page. We investigate within 5 business days. If funds were misused or the campaign was fraudulent, we refund you from our guarantee reserve.

Covers gifts made on Madinah.com and in the app.

Claims open for 12 months after your gift.

Full terms: madinah.com/guarantee

What it costs

Madinah charges no platform fee. Card companies charge a processing fee on every payment, which you can choose to cover.

EXAMPLE: YOU GIVE $100
Your gift$100.00
Madinah platform fee$0.00
Card processing (about 2.9% + $0.30)−$3.20
The charity receives$96.80

Tick “cover the fees” and the charity gets the full $100.
